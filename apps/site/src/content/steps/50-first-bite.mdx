<Claim id="first-bite-syndrome">Some people feel a sharp cramping pain in the cheek with the first bite of each meal, easing over the next bites. It followed 6% of operations for benign tumours in one series and was much more common after surgery on the deep part of the gland in another.</Claim>

<Claim id="first-bite-syndrome">It is thought to arise from loss of the sympathetic nerve supply to the parotid.</Claim>

<Model>The sympathetic supply is not modelled, so the patch marks only where the pain is felt.</Model>
