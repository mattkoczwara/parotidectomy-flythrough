<Claim id="antegrade-dissection">Surgeons usually find the main trunk first, then follow it forward.</Claim> Several landmarks point to it: <Claim id="landmark-tympanomastoid-suture">a seam in the bone below the ear canal, which the trunk lies just deep to;</Claim> <Claim id="landmark-posterior-belly-digastric">the upper edge of a small neck muscle, the digastric;</Claim> <Claim id="landmark-tragal-pointer">and the cartilage of the ear canal, a less exact guide because it moves.</Claim> <Claim id="landmark-styloid">The trunk passes just outside a thin spur of bone, the styloid.</Claim>

In this view the skin and gland are cut away; in surgery they are lifted as a flap.
